FaithXFitness

Bring faith and fitness into the same rhythm with Scripture between sets, guided strength workouts, and outdoor run tracking.

Download FaithXFitness on the App Store

Scripture between sets

Read or listen to supported Scripture while your rest timer runs, then return to your workout. Save verses for later, continue with reading plans and devotionals, or write in a private reflection journal.

Strength workouts you can follow

Choose a guided routine or build a custom workout. Watch exercise demonstrations, review the muscles involved, and record weights, repetitions, sets, and completed sessions.

Run, reflect, and share when you choose

Track outdoor distance, elapsed time, pace, and your route. After a run, create a recap with selected stats and Scripture, then choose whether to share it through your iPhone.

Faith throughout your day

Explore a curated offline Scripture library, daily verses, themed feeds, Home Screen widgets, optional reminders, and a private prayer and reflection journal.
