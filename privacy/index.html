Privacy policy

Last updated August 31, 2026

FaithXFitness helps you build a sustainable rhythm of faith and fitness. We collect only what is needed to provide the app: profile choices, workout and reading progress, and account information when you choose to sign in.

Data we use

Your data is stored on your device. If you create an account, selected data is backed up through our hosted service so it can follow you to another device. Purchases are processed by Apple. Health data is accessed only after you grant permission and is used to provide the feature you enable.

Your choices

You can change permissions in iOS Settings, export or delete your account from the app, and cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings. We do not sell your personal information.

Contact

For privacy questions, use FaithXFitness Support.
