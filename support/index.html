FaithXFitness

Support

For account, subscription, privacy, audio, workout, or community-safety help, email support@faithxfitness.app. Include your device model and app build number. Never send your password, payment details, or an entire journal.

Report or block someone

In the updated app, open the three-dot safety menu beside a community profile or received message. Choose a reason and Send report, or Block this person. Reports go to a private review queue. If a report cannot be sent, email support with the username and enough context to investigate; share only what is necessary. Manage blocks from Community → Blocked people. For immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Account data

Delete account in Settings removes active server data and the account backup. Delete all data on this phone removes local records only. To remove both, delete the account first. Contact support for data-access requests or help with deletion.

Purchases and audio

Use Restore purchases from the membership screen. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For reading audio, check device volume and the selected voice, then retry Read aloud.
